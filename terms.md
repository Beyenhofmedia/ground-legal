# Terms of Use — Ground

**Last updated: 9 October 2026**


## What Ground is

Ground is a prayer app. You tell God how you are; it gives you a verse from
the Bible and a prayer, and keeps a garden and a journal of what you have
prayed.

**Ground is not a counsellor, a therapist, a pastor or a medical service.**
It cannot help in an emergency. If you are in danger, call your local
emergency number. In the United States, the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline can
be reached by calling or texting 988.

## No account

Ground has no account. There is nothing to sign up for and no password. A
subscription belongs to your Apple ID and is managed there.

You must be 13 or older to use Ground.

## Subscriptions

Ground is free for the first ten days. After that, Ground asks for a
subscription, offered as a yearly, a monthly or a weekly plan at the prices
shown in the app. **Nothing is taken away if you decide not to subscribe**;
the app keeps working, and the subscription is simply what keeps Ground
going.

- **Payment is charged to your Apple ID** at confirmation of purchase, or at
  the end of the free trial if you started one.
- **It renews automatically** unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24
  hours before the end of the current period.
- **Your Apple ID is charged for renewal** within 24 hours before the end of
  the current period, at the price of the plan you chose.
- **You manage and cancel it in your Apple ID settings**, not in Ground. We
  cannot cancel it for you.
- **Any unused part of a free trial is forfeited** if you buy a subscription
  during it.

Prices may change. If they do, you will be told before a renewal at the new
price, and you can cancel.

## Refunds

Purchases are made through Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple, not by us.
Request one at https://reportaproblem.apple.com.

## What you write

**Your prayers are yours.** They are stored on your device, we do not receive
them, and nothing in these terms gives us any claim over them. You can take
the whole journal with you or delete every word of it at any time, in
Settings, with or without a subscription.

## What you send to others

Ground can hand things to your phone's share sheet when you ask: the words of
an answered prayer, a seed link that plants your prayer in a friend's garden,
a picture of your season, or your journal as a file. You choose what to send,
to whom, and through which app. Do not send a seed to somebody who has not
asked for one, and do not put another person's private words into a seed.

## The Companion

The Companion is an **AI**. It is not a person, a pastor or a counsellor, and
it can be wrong. What you send it is passed to our AI provider to be
answered and is not kept. Do not rely on it for medical, legal, financial or
emergency help. If an answer is wrong or harmful, use *Report this answer*
under it to tell us.

Scripture shown anywhere in Ground comes from the bundled Bible text and is
never written by the AI.

## Things you agree not to do

- Use Ground to break the law, or to harm or harass anybody.
- Try to break, overload or reverse-engineer the app or its servers.
- Resell or redistribute the app or its contents.

## Ending it

You can stop using Ground at any time; cancel the subscription in your Apple
ID settings and delete the app. We may end or suspend access to the Companion
relay if these terms are broken.

the law allows, our total liability to you is limited to what you paid us in

Nothing here limits liability that cannot be limited by law.
